# AI Cost Allocation

## Overview

AI Cost Allocation is the process of assigning AI consumption and costs to accountable business entities.

Without allocation, organizations can measure total AI spending but cannot determine ownership or accountability.

Allocation enables:

- Visibility
- Accountability
- Showback
- Chargeback
- Forecasting
- Optimization

---

## Why AI Cost Allocation Matters

Organizations often begin with a single AI platform account.

Over time, multiple teams consume AI services:

- Customer Support
- Engineering
- Product
- Marketing
- Finance

Without allocation, AI spending becomes a shared expense with no clear ownership.

---

## Allocation Hierarchy

### Business Unit Allocation

Examples:

- Sales
- Marketing
- Engineering
- Customer Success

Measure:

- Token Consumption
- Inference Volume
- AI Spend

---

### Application Allocation

Examples:

- Support Bot
- Engineering Copilot
- Knowledge Assistant

Measure:

- Cost per Application
- Token Consumption by Application
- Cost Trends

---

### Workflow Allocation

Examples:

- Ticket Resolution
- Customer Onboarding
- Sales Proposal Generation

Measure:

- Cost per Workflow
- Cost per Transaction
- Cost per Business Process

---

### Owner Allocation

Assign ownership to:

- Product Owners
- Engineering Teams
- Business Leaders

Every AI workload should have an accountable owner.

---

## Shared Cost Allocation

Some AI costs benefit multiple teams.

Examples:

- Shared RAG Systems
- Shared Vector Databases
- Shared AI Platforms

Allocation methods may include:

- Equal Distribution
- Usage-Based Allocation
- Consumption-Based Allocation

---

## AI Showback

Showback provides visibility into AI spending without financial chargeback.

Example:

Engineering:
- $5,000

Customer Success:
- $3,000

Marketing:
- $2,000

The objective is awareness and accountability.

---

## AI Chargeback

Chargeback allocates actual AI costs back to consuming teams.

Examples:

- Cost Center Billing
- Department Billing
- Product Billing

The objective is financial accountability.

---

## Business Outcome

AI Cost Allocation transforms AI spending from a shared expense into an accountable business investment.
