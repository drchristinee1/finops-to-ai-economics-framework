# AI Investment Governance

## Overview

AI Investment Governance applies FinOps operating principles to AI investments.

The objective is not simply to manage token spend.

The objective is to ensure AI investments generate measurable business value while maintaining financial accountability and operational efficiency.

---

## Why AI Investment Governance Matters

Organizations adopting AI often focus on:

- Model selection
- Prompt engineering
- Application development

However, long-term success requires governance over:

- AI spending
- AI consumption growth
- Business value realization
- Investment prioritization
- Portfolio management

Without governance, AI costs can grow faster than business value.

---

## Key Governance Questions

### What happened?

Measure:

- AI spend
- Token consumption
- Inference volume
- Cost by application
- Cost by business unit

### Why did it happen?

Analyze:

- Demand growth
- Context expansion
- Model selection
- New workloads
- Increased user adoption

### Who owns it?

Assign accountability to:

- Applications
- Business Units
- Product Teams
- Workflow Owners

### What should we do?

Identify actions such as:

- Model optimization
- Context reduction
- Prompt optimization
- Caching
- Commitment purchases
- Provider optimization

### Did it work?

Validate:

- Cost reduction
- Quality retention
- ROI improvement
- Business outcome achievement

---

## AI Portfolio Management

AI should be managed as a portfolio of investments.

Examples:

| AI Investment | Business Objective |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Customer Support Bot | Reduce support costs |
| Engineering Copilot | Improve developer productivity |
| Sales Assistant | Increase revenue |
| Knowledge Assistant | Improve employee efficiency |

Each investment should have:

- Owner
- Budget
- KPIs
- ROI targets
- Governance reviews

---

## AI Commitment Management

As AI consumption stabilizes, organizations may purchase:

- Reserved Capacity
- Provisioned Throughput
- Volume Commitments
- Enterprise Agreements

Governance should monitor:

- Coverage
- Utilization
- Discount Capture
- Commitment Risk

## Business Outcome

AI Investment Governance transforms AI spending into a managed portfolio of business investments.

The goal is not simply lower cost.

The goal is maximizing business value per dollar invested.
