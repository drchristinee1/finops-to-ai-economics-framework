# AI Economics

## Overview

AI Economics is the application of FinOps operating principles to AI consumption.

While traditional cloud economics focuses on compute, storage, databases, and networking, AI Economics focuses on:

- Input Tokens
- Output Tokens
- Context Windows
- Inference Volume
- Model Consumption
- Latency
- Quality

The telemetry changes from compute and storage to tokens and inferences, but the economic questions remain the same.

---

## Fundamental AI FinOps Questions

1. What happened?
2. Why did it happen?
3. Who owns it?
4. What should we do?
5. Did it work?

---

## Core AI Economics Capabilities

### AI Cost Allocation

Map AI costs to:

- Applications
- Business Units
- Workflows
- Owners

### AI Showback

Provide visibility into AI consumption.

### AI Chargeback

Allocate AI costs to accountable teams.

### AI Forecasting

Predict future token and inference spend.

### AI Optimization

Identify opportunities to reduce AI costs while maintaining quality.

### AI Unit Economics

Measure:

- Cost per Request
- Cost per Workflow
- Cost per User
- Cost per Business Outcome

### AI ROI

Connect AI spending to business value.

---

## Business Outcome

AI Economics transforms model telemetry into business accountability, optimization, and investment governance.
