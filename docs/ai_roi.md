# AI ROI

## Overview

AI ROI (Return on Investment) measures the business value generated from AI investments relative to the cost required to operate those AI systems.

While traditional AI reporting often focuses on token consumption and model costs, AI ROI focuses on business outcomes.

The objective is to determine whether AI investments create measurable value for the organization.

---

## Why AI ROI Matters

Organizations cannot optimize what they cannot value.

Without ROI measurement:

- AI spending appears as a cost center
- Investment decisions become subjective
- Business leaders lack confidence in AI initiatives
- Cost optimization efforts lose business context

AI ROI connects AI spending to business outcomes.

---

## Core AI ROI Questions

Organizations need to understand:

1. What are we spending on AI?
2. What value are we receiving?
3. Which AI initiatives create the most value?
4. Which workloads should receive additional investment?
5. Which workloads should be optimized or retired?

## Basic ROI Formula

ROI = (Business Value - AI Cost) / AI Cost

Example:

Business Value = $600,000

AI Cost = $100,000

ROI = ($600,000 - $100,000) / $100,000

ROI = 5.0

ROI = 500%

## Relationship to AI Unit Economics

AI Unit Economics measures:

- Cost per Request
- Cost per Workflow
- Cost per User

- Value per Request
