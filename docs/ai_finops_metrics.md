# AI FinOps Metrics

## Overview

AI FinOps relies on measurable operational and economic metrics.

The objective is not simply monitoring token consumption.

The objective is measuring efficiency, accountability, optimization, and business value.

---

## Consumption Metrics

### Average Input Tokens

Measures:

Average prompt size submitted to AI models.

Why it matters:

Input tokens drive AI costs.

---

### Average Output Tokens

Measures:

Average response size generated by AI models.

Why it matters:

Output tokens often represent a major cost driver.

---

### Context Percentage

Formula:

Context Tokens ÷ Total Input Tokens

Why it matters:

Measures how much of a request consists of context.

High percentages may indicate inefficiency.

---

### Context Growth Rate

Measures:

Monthly growth in context size.

Why it matters:

Detects prompt bloat and increasing costs.

---

## Cost Metrics

### Cost per Request

Formula:

Total AI Cost ÷ Total Requests

---

### Cost per Workflow

Formula:

Total Workflow Cost ÷ Workflow Executions

---

### Cost per User

Formula:

Total AI Cost ÷ Active Users

---

## Optimization Metrics

### Cache Hit Rate

Formula:

Cached Requests ÷ Total Requests

Why it matters:

Higher cache rates reduce AI costs.

---

### Retrieval Efficiency

Measures:

Effectiveness of retrieval systems.

Why it matters:

Efficient retrieval reduces context size and cost.

---

### Model Routing Efficiency

Measures:

Savings achieved through intelligent model selection.

---

## Performance Metrics

### Latency

Measures:

Response Time

Examples:

- Time to First Token
- Total Response Time

---

### Quality Score

Measures:

Output effectiveness.

Examples:

- User Ratings
- Success Rates
- Accuracy Scores

---

## Financial Metrics

### AI ROI

Formula:

Business Value ÷ AI Cost

Examples:

- Labor Savings
- Revenue Growth
- Productivity Gains

---
