# AI Forecasting

## Overview

AI Forecasting is the practice of predicting future AI consumption, token usage, inference demand, and associated costs.

Just as cloud forecasting estimates future compute, storage, and network consumption, AI forecasting estimates future model consumption and AI spending.

The objective is to provide organizations with visibility into expected AI costs before they occur.

---

## Why AI Forecasting Matters

Without forecasting, organizations:

- Lose budget predictability
- Struggle to evaluate AI investments
- Cannot make informed commitment decisions
- Experience unexpected cost growth
- Lack confidence in AI adoption planning

AI Forecasting creates financial visibility and governance for AI consumption.

---

## Core Forecasting Questions

Organizations need to understand:

1. How much AI spend should we expect next month?
2. What workloads are driving growth?
3. Which business units are increasing consumption?
4. Which applications generate the highest AI costs?
5. How much consumption is predictable versus experimental?
6. What commitments should we purchase?

## Baseline vs Variable Demand

One of the most important forecasting concepts is separating stable demand from variable demand.

### Baseline Demand

Predictable consumption:

- Production applications
- Established workflows
- Recurring business processes

### Variable Demand

Unpredictable consumption:

- New applications
- Pilot programs
- Experimental AI initiatives
- Seasonal spikes

Forecast accuracy improves when baseline and variable demand are modeled separately.

---

## Forecasting Methods

### Trend-Based Forecasting

Uses historical growth rates.

Example:

January: 10M tokens

February: 12M tokens

March: 14M tokens

Expected April consumption based on trend growth.

---

### Driver-Based Forecasting

Uses business drivers.

Example:

Cost per AI request = $0.01

Expected requests = 5 million

Forecast Cost:

Forecast Cost = Cost per Request × Request Volume

---

### Scenario Forecasting

Models multiple outcomes.

Scenarios:

- Conservative
- Expected
- Aggressive

This helps leadership evaluate risk.
