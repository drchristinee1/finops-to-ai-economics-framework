# AI Unit Economics

## Overview

AI Unit Economics measures the relationship between AI consumption, cost, and business value.

While Tokenomics measures consumption and Context Economics measures context efficiency, AI Unit Economics connects AI spend to outcomes.

The objective is answering:

- Is AI creating value?
- What does that value cost?
- Is the investment economically justified?

---

## Why Unit Economics Matters

Organizations do not invest in tokens.

Organizations invest in business outcomes.

Examples:

- Faster customer support
- Increased sales productivity
- Reduced engineering effort
- Improved marketing output
- Accelerated financial analysis

AI Unit Economics quantifies those outcomes.

---

## Core AI Unit Metrics

### Cost per Request

AI Spend ÷ Requests

Measures operational efficiency.

---

### Cost per Workflow

AI Spend ÷ Workflow Volume

Examples:

- Cost per proposal
- Cost per ticket summarized
- Cost per code review
- Cost per report generated

---

### Cost per User

AI Spend ÷ Active Users

Measures adoption efficiency.

---

### Cost per Inference

AI Spend ÷ Total Inferences

Measures model efficiency.

---

### Cost per Business Outcome

AI Spend ÷ Outcomes Generated

Examples:

- Cost per sale
- Cost per support case resolved
- Cost per engineering task completed

---

## Value Metrics

### Hours Saved

Measure labor reduction generated by AI.

---

### Revenue Impact

Measure incremental revenue generated by AI.

---

### Productivity Gain

Measure increased output from employees.

---

### Quality Improvement

Measure improvement in accuracy or customer satisfaction.

---

## AI ROI

AI ROI =

(Value Generated − AI Spend) ÷ AI Spend

A positive ROI indicates value exceeds cost.

---

## Optimization Opportunities

- Model Routing
- Prompt Optimization

- Response Length Controls
- Caching
- Fine Tuning
- Provider Selection

All optimization efforts should improve unit economics.

---

## Business Outcome

AI Unit Economics transforms token consumption into measurable business value, enabling investment decisions, prioritization, and executive governance.
