# AI Commitment Management

## Overview

AI Commitment Management is the practice of purchasing, monitoring, and optimizing AI capacity commitments to reduce costs while minimizing financial risk.

Just as Cloud FinOps uses Savings Plans, Reserved Instances, and Committed Use Discounts, AI Economics increasingly relies on provider commitments, reserved capacity, and volume agreements.

The objective is maximizing discount capture while maintaining flexibility.

---

## Why AI Commitment Management Matters

As AI adoption grows, organizations often move from:

- On-Demand Consumption

to

- Committed Consumption

Commitments can significantly reduce costs but introduce financial risk if consumption does not materialize as expected.

AI Commitment Management balances savings and risk.

---

## Core Commitment Questions

Organizations need to understand:

1. How much AI demand is predictable?
2. How much demand is experimental?
3. What commitment level is appropriate?
4. Are commitments being fully utilized?
5. Are we overcommitted or undercommitted?

---

## Baseline vs Variable Demand

The most important commitment principle is separating stable demand from variable demand.

### Baseline Demand

Predictable consumption:

- Production applications
- Established workflows
- Recurring business processes

Examples:

- Customer Support Bots
- Internal Copilots
- Enterprise Knowledge Assistants

---

### Variable Demand

Uncertain consumption:

- Pilots
- Experiments
- New product launches
- Seasonal spikes

Variable demand should generally remain on-demand.

---

## Commitment Types

Examples include:

### Reserved Capacity

Dedicated model capacity purchased in advance.

Benefits:

- Lower unit cost
- Improved performance predictability

---

### Provisioned Throughput

Pre-purchased inference capacity.

Benefits:

- Guaranteed throughput
- Lower effective pricing

---

### Volume Agreements

Commitments based on expected usage volume.

Benefits:

- Discounted pricing
- Predictable spend

---

### Enterprise Commitments

Negotiated annual consumption agreements.

Benefits:

- Strategic pricing
- Long-term discount capture

---

## Core Commitment Metrics

### Coverage

Measures:

Committed Consumption ÷ Total Consumption

Example:

70M committed tokens

100M total tokens

Coverage:

70%

---

### Utilization

Measures:

Consumed Commitments ÷ Purchased Commitments

Example:

90M consumed

100M purchased

Utilization:

90%

---

### Discount Capture

Measures:

Savings generated through commitments.

Example:

On-Demand Cost:

$1,000,000

Committed Cost:

$800,000

Discount Capture:

$200,000

---

### Commitment Risk

Measures:

Unused commitments.

Examples:

- Over-purchased capacity
- Underutilized agreements

---

## Commitment Strategy

A common AI FinOps approach:

1. Establish consumption baseline
2. Separate stable demand from growth demand
3. Commit conservatively
4. Monitor utilization monthly
5. Expand commitments gradually

The goal is maximizing savings while minimizing commitment risk.

---

## Relationship to AI Forecasting

Forecasting drives commitment decisions.

Without reliable forecasts:

- Commitments become risky
- Utilization declines
- Financial waste increases

Forecasting and Commitment Management should operate together.

---

## Executive Dashboard Metrics

A mature AI commitment program tracks:

- Commitment Coverage
- Commitment Utilization
- Discount Capture
- Commitment Risk
- Forecast Variance
- Committed Spend
- On-Demand Spend
- Effective Cost per Request

---

## Business Outcome

AI Commitment Management transforms predictable AI demand into financial savings by balancing discount capture, utilization, flexibility, and risk.
